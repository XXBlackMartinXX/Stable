# Consultant Handoff Brief

**For a licensed architect / engineering team taking this concept forward.**

## What you are receiving

A validated schematic layout for a 40 × 50 m luxury equestrian stable: a resolved room program,
exact dimensions, a dimensioned plan, a CAD underlay, complete schedules, and an illustrated master
plan. The design intent and geometry are sound and internally consistent.

## Start with

1. `05_TECHNICAL_REPORT/source_of_truth_summary.md` — the locked facts and owner decisions.
2. `03_TECHNICAL_DRAWINGS/cad_underlay.dxf` — coordinated CAD reference (concept underlay only).
3. `04_SCHEDULES/room_schedule.csv` — every room's exact size, position, and function.
4. `production_readiness_gap_checklist.md` (this folder) — what is done vs. what your team must
   produce.

## What is not done (your scope)

No structural, MEP, civil/drainage, fire/life-safety, zoning/code, or site-survey work has been
performed. No door/window schedules, wall sections, foundation details, or cost estimate exist.
These are the disciplines to quote next. The gap checklist lists them in full.

## Photoreal visualization

If photoreal renders are required, use `stable_scene.obj`/`.mtl` (this folder) with
`render_production_brief.md`, `camera_schedule.csv`, `material_palette.csv`, and
`render_view_checklist.md`. Do not change the validated layout when rendering.

*Concept plan only. Not for construction or permit use until reviewed and approved by licensed
professionals.*
